An Exploratory Data Analysis project to understand customer purchasing behavior and sales patterns during Diwali using Python.

---

## 🎯 Objective
Analyze customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
Identify the top-performing states.
Understand purchasing patterns based on age, gender, marital status, and occupation.
Identify the most popular product categories.
Find the key customer segment contributing to Diwali sales.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
🐍 Python
🐼 Pandas
🔢 NumPy
📊 Matplotlib
🎨 Seaborn

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning
Removed unnecessary columns such as Status and unnamed1.
Checked and removed missing values.
Converted the Amount column to integer format.
Performed descriptive statistical analysis.

---

## 📊 EDA & Key Insights
👩 Females form a major portion of the buyers.
🎯 The 26–35 years age group has the highest number of buyers.
📍 Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka have the highest number of orders.
💍 Married women show high purchasing power.
💼 Buyers working in IT, Healthcare, and Aviation contribute significantly to sales.
🛍️ Food, Clothing & Apparel, and Electronics are among the highest-selling product categories.

---

## 💡 Conclusion
The analysis shows that married women aged 26–35 from Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka, particularly those working in IT, Healthcare, and Aviation, represent an important customer segment. Food, Clothing & Apparel, and Electronics are the leading product categories during Diwali.
